# UP Factory Story

**The factory state that is speeding up.** A magazine-style data story on Uttar Pradesh's factories, built from the Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) 2024-25 and 2023-24, published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).

Read it: https://up-factory-story.netlify.app/

## What's inside

Seven short chapters, each built around one number:

1. **Scale (#4)**: UP ranks fourth in India for factories, jobs, output and inputs.
2. **Speed (+18.9%)**: the fastest output growth of the 16 big factory States in 2024-25.
3. **Efficiency (₹4.28)**: output for every ₹1 of fixed capital, against ₹3.23 for India.
4. **What UP makes (#1 in 4)**: first in food products, beverages, tobacco and publishing.
5. **People (18.3 lakh)**: who works in UP's factories, and what they earn.
6. **Money (₹69,336 cr)**: investment, power and fuel, and borrowing.
7. **Headroom (₹46,731 cr)**: what closing the productivity gap with India would add.

An appendix has a tile map of all States and a searchable table of every UP figure used.

## Sources and notes
